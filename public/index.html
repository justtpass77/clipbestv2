Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers about how ClipBest processes streams, licensing, and export policies.

Why is ClipBest priced per video instead of per minute?

Competitors charge by the minute of footage uploaded. If you upload a 4-hour livestream, you burn through your entire monthly plan in one go. ClipBest downloads and processes only the selected peak engagement moments, keeping operational costs flat and passing the savings directly to you.

How does heatmap-based clip detection work?

We query YouTube and Kick playback retention telemetry. The moments where thousands of real viewers rewound, rewatched, or clipped are mathematically proven to deliver the highest retention. If telemetry is unavailable, our audio energy analyzer scans for vocal spikes, crowd cheers, and game sound effects.

Can I upload recordings directly from my hard drive?

Yes. Click the "Upload Video File" tab in the composer. We support MP4, MOV, MKV, and WebM video files up to 1 GB with offline hardware encoding.

Which streaming platforms are supported?

We support public YouTube streams and VODs, Twitch VODs, and Kick recordings. You can paste any valid stream link or upload the video directly.

What video file formats are supported?

We support MP4, MOV (QuickTime), MKV, and WebM inputs. Outputs are rendered in universal H.264 / AAC MP4 format calibrated for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.

How does automatic vertical reframing and streamer split work?

The layout engine analyzes the original 16:9 stream coordinates. For streamer split, it isolates the corner webcam quadrant and center gameplay, stacking them vertically into standard 9:16 resolution with zero distortion.

How do kinetic subtitles work and can I customize colors?

Audio is transcribed and compiled into frame-accurate SSA/ASS subtitles using libass. You can choose from three typography styles (Classic Yellow, Impact Green, Podcast Pill) and customize the active word highlight color.

What are the differences between pricing plans?

Free Starter gives you 3 clips per video with a subtle watermark. Creator Pro ($9/mo) removes all watermarks, unlocks up to 10 clips per video, enables streamer split screen, 1-click ZIP export, and burns your custom social handle. Clipper Agency ($19/mo) unlocks up to 20 clips per video, CSV scheduler manifests for Buffer/TikTok, and commercial client resale rights.

Are generated videos watermarked on the free tier?

Free tier exports include a small ClipBest watermark badge in the corner. Upgrading to Creator Pro or Clipper Agency removes all watermarks and lets you burn your own social handle (e.g. @FaZe_Clan) into all clips.

Can I download and use generated clips commercially?

Yes. Any clips generated on Creator Pro or Clipper Agency plans include full commercial usage rights for social media publishing, client work, Whop campaigns, and monetized creator channels.
