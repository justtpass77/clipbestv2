# Clipbest — Income & Pricing Plan

*Written 19 Nov 2026. Competitor numbers come from third-party comparison pages and change often — re-check before launch.*

---

## 1. The one-line pitch

**"Paste a stream, get the best clips in 90 seconds. Priced per video, not per minute — so a 4-hour stream costs the same as a 10-minute one."**

That last part is the wedge. Every big competitor charges by minutes of footage uploaded. Streamers and clippers work with 2–6 hour VODs, so minute-based credits burn out fast. Clipbest only downloads the selected moments, so our cost per video is nearly flat — we can afford to price per video and still make money.

## 2. Who actually pays (in order of priority)

| Buyer | Why they pay | What they need most |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Clippers on paid campaigns** (Whop Content Rewards etc.) | They earn per view (median ~$1 per 1,000 views on gaming campaigns). More clips posted = more money, so a tool that saves time pays for itself. | Volume, speed, no watermark, many clips per stream, 9:16 + captions |
| **2. Small/mid streamers & YouTubers** (gaming, GTA RP) | Want Shorts/TikTok growth without hiring an editor | One-click clips from their own VODs, brand style, auto zoom, hooks |
| **3. Clipping agencies / page owners** | Run several accounts, post daily | Bulk links, priority speed, brand kits, team seats |
| Free users / students / viewers | Mostly won't pay — they're your marketing (shares, watermark, word of mouth) | Free tier that actually works |

**Focus on #1 first.** Their return is easy to show: at ~$1 per 1,000 views, a $9 plan pays for itself after ~9,000 views. That's one decent clip.

## 3. What people pay for vs. what they hate

### Features people pay for (ranked)
1. **No watermark** — the #1 reason anyone upgrades from free in this category.
2. **More clips per video** (free = 3, paid = all of them) — already built: locked "Pro clip" cards show exactly what they're missing.
3. **Long videos/streams with no minute limits** — our unique advantage.
4. **Caption styles + brand look** (fonts, colours, hook titles) — built (3 styles + 7 colours). Pro: custom fonts/logo.
5. **Speed / priority queue** — built (~90 s per job). Pro gets their jobs processed first when the server is busy.
6. **AI hooks, captions & hashtags** — built (Gemini free; users can bring their own key).
7. **Formats + auto zoom** (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9, Fill + punch-in) — built.
8. **Next to build:** face tracking crop for facecam streams, bulk paste (10 links at once), "download all" ZIP, auto-posting to TikTok/YouTube/IG, upload your own file, Twitch VOD support.

### What users hate about competitors → turn into our selling points
| Pain (from reviews/comparisons) | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Credits charged **per minute** — long streams eat the whole plan | **Per-video pricing, any length** |
| Credits **expire** (60 days on monthly plans at OpusClip) | Credit packs **never expire** |
| Card required for trial / auto-renew traps | **No sign-up for free, no card** |
| Slow queues | Results in ~90 seconds |
| Bad clip picks | Uses real "most replayed" viewer data + shows a virality score |

## 4. Pricing (recommended)

Competitor reference points: OpusClip Free → $15 Starter → $29 Pro (minute-based credits); Vizard/Submagic ~$16 entry; Klap ~$29; Munch ~$49. **Undercut on price, beat them on the per-video model.**

| Plan | Price | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | 3 clips per video · 5 videos per month · small "Made with Clipbest" watermark · all formats · basic + Gemini hooks |
| **Creator** | **$9 / month** ($84/yr = $7/mo) | Up to 10 clips per video · 40 videos/month · **no watermark** · all caption styles & colours · auto zoom · AI hooks |
| **Clipper Pro** | **$19 / month** ($180/yr = $15/mo) | Up to 20 clips per video · 150 videos/month · priority queue · bulk links · ZIP download · custom font/logo · (later: face tracking, auto-post) |
| **Pay-as-you-go pack** | **$5 = 10 videos** (never expire) | For occasional users and price-sensitive countries. No subscription. |

Why these numbers:
- **$9** is under every competitor's entry plan, and an easy "yes" for a clipper earning per view.
- **$19 for heavy users** is still cheaper than OpusClip Pro ($29) and has no minute limits.
- **Credit packs** catch people who refuse subscriptions, and they also cover markets where $9/month is a lot. You can also offer **regional pricing** (e.g. ~40–50% off in India/LATAM) through your payment provider.
- **Annual = 2 months free.** Annual plans bring cash in up front and cut churn.

**Launch offer (first 30 days only):** "Founding member" Creator plan at **$5/month locked for life**, capped at the first 200 users. That creates urgency and early reviews without a risky unlimited lifetime deal (video processing has real compute cost).

## 5. What makes people love it (retention)

- **The first result is good.** The first 90 seconds decide everything, so keep the default settings excellent (9:16, Classic captions, AI hooks).
- **Show value before the paywall.** Free users see the locked clips with their scores and timestamps, so they know exactly what they'd get.
- **Remember their style.** Already built: saved settings per browser. Next: accounts with saved brand presets.
- **Weekly "your best moments" email** for streamers who link their channel (later).
- **Honest limits.** No hidden expiry, clear usage meter.

## 6. Getting users at $0 marketing budget

1. **Use your own tool.** Run 2–3 GTA 6 clipping pages (TikTok, Shorts, IG) with Clipbest clips, and join paid campaigns. You earn per view *and* get proof ("this clip got 400k views, made with Clipbest").
2. **Watermark = free ads.** Every free clip that gets posted advertises the tool.
3. **Go where clippers are:** Whop/Content Rewards communities, clipping Discords, r/NewTubers, r/GamingContentCreators, GTA RP Discords. Post helpful guides, not spam.
4. **Affiliate program:** 30% recurring commission for clippers who refer others. Clippers already run audiences.
5. **SEO pages** (cheap, compounding): "GTA 6 clip maker", "free OpusClip alternative", "Twitch stream to TikTok", "clip long streams no minute limit".
6. **Launch posts:** Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, X/Twitter build-in-public threads with before/after clips.

## 7. Realistic money math (be honest with yourself)

| Stage | Free users / month | Paid (3–5% convert) | Monthly revenue (avg ~$11) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1–2 | 300–1,000 | 10–40 | **$100–450** |
| Month 3–6 | 2,000–5,000 | 60–250 | **$650–2,750** |
| Good case, 12 months | 10,000+ | 300–500 | **$3,300–5,500** |

It is not instantly passive. The first months are building, posting and talking to users. It becomes *mostly* passive once marketing runs through SEO, affiliates and the watermark loop.

**Costs as you grow** (still close to $0 at the start):
- **Server:** your PC now → Oracle Cloud Always Free ARM VM → a ~$20–40/mo dedicated box once you have ~100 paying users. Video rendering is the main cost.
- **AI hooks:** Gemini free tier now. At scale, a paid Flash-Lite model costs fractions of a cent per video.
- **Payments:** use a "merchant of record" (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy or similar). They handle global sales tax and VAT for ~5% + a small fee per sale, with no monthly fee. Check which one supports payouts in your country.
- **Storage:** auto-delete clips after 24–48 hours (tell users to download).

## 8. Big risks — fix these before charging money

1. **YouTube downloading.** YouTube's terms don't allow downloading videos, and yt-dlp breaks whenever YouTube changes. Payment providers can **refuse or freeze accounts** for "YouTube downloader" products. Mitigation:
   - Position it as a **clipping tool for content you own or have permission to use** (streamers' own VODs, authorized clipping campaigns). Keep that clear in the terms and UI.
   - **Add "upload your own video file" and Twitch/Kick VOD support** so the business doesn't depend on one source.
2. **Copyright.** Clips of other creators' streams or Rockstar trailers can get users' accounts struck. Campaigns on Whop are fine (the creator asked for clips). Put a clear "you must have rights" checkbox in the terms.
3. **Running on your PC.** If the PC is off, the site is down, and one PC can only process a few jobs at once. Move to a cloud server before charging.
4. **Pro codes are temporary.** Replace them with real accounts + license keys from the payment provider before launch.
